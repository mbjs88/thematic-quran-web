Privacy Policy

Information regarding data collection, usage, and your privacy rights.

security Data Collection & Authentication

Thematic Qur'an operates on a strict data-minimization model. We do not require you to create a separate account or password to use our personalized features. Instead, we utilize OAuth 2.0 to securely authenticate you via the Quran Foundation API (Quran.com).

  • arrow_right Authentication occurs directly on Quran.com's secure servers.
  • arrow_right We receive a standard access token that allows us to retrieve your study data.
  • arrow_right This data is fetched dynamically and processed at the network edge, rather than being stored in a centralized database managed by us.

dataset Data Usage

The information accessed via your authentication token is utilized solely to provide core service functionality:

  • arrow_right Synchronizing audio listening progress with your Quran.com reading history.
  • arrow_right Saving bookmarks and custom audio mixes between sessions.
  • arrow_right Maintaining continuity for notes and study streaks across both platforms.

We do not use your information for advertising purposes, nor do we sell or share your data with third-party brokers.

rate_review Feedback Submissions

If you send feedback, we collect the category you choose, your message, and an optional email address if you want a reply. You may also choose to include page context such as the current page, view mode, active Theme Search query, browser, viewport, language, and timezone.

Feedback is separate from your Quran.com account data. It is used only to triage bugs, improve the site, and respond when you provide contact details.

dns Infrastructure & Third Parties

We rely on industry-standard providers to host our application and ensure reliable performance:

Cloudflare

Our frontend and network routing are managed via Cloudflare Pages and Workers. Cloudflare processes standard network data (e.g., IP addresses) necessary for security, caching, and traffic distribution.

The Quran Foundation

Integration with the Quran.com API facilitates user profile management. Data persisted on their infrastructure is governed by their respective privacy and security policies.

no_accounts Revoking Access

You maintain full control over your data connection:

  • 1
    Session Logout Logging out on Thematic Qur'an immediately clears the active OAuth token from your browser session.
  • 2
    Global Permissions You can permanently revoke application access by visiting your account settings directly on Quran.com.
